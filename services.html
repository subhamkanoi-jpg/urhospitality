Home Services

Services built for the complexity of corporate food.

From daily cafeteria management to large-scale events — every service designed around operational reliability, food safety and employee satisfaction.

01 — Managed Corporate Cafeteria

Your cafeteria, our operation.

End-to-end management of your workplace cafeteria — menu planning, procurement, cooking, service and cleanup.

  • Dedicated on-site team
  • Daily menu rotation
  • Dietary customisation (Jain, vegan, regional)
  • Hygiene & safety management
  • Monthly performance reports
The Benefit

Your admin team stops managing food. Your employees start enjoying it.

02 — Corporate Meal Programs

Structured meals for structured workdays.

Recurring breakfast, lunch, snacks, and dinner programs designed around your workforce's schedule, dietary preferences and budget.

  • Multi-cuisine daily menus
  • Flexible meal configurations
  • Headcount-based portioning
  • Real-time feedback integration
  • Budget-aligned pricing
The Benefit

Consistent quality across every meal, every day.

03 — Corporate Events

Event food with cafeteria-grade reliability.

Town halls, leadership meetings, annual events, launches, and celebrations — catered with the same operational discipline as daily service.

  • Custom event menus
  • Live counters & themed stations
  • Professional service staff
  • Event-day coordination
  • Post-event cleanup
The Benefit

The food matches the occasion. The logistics disappear.

04 — Executive & Boardroom Catering

Premium format. Zero premium headache.

Smaller-format premium corporate dining for board meetings, executive lunches and VIP hosting. High touch presentation with completely discreet service.

  • Curated premium menus
  • Plated or buffet service
  • Professional presentation
  • Dietary accommodation
  • Discreet on-site service
The Benefit

Your boardroom gets restaurant quality without restaurant complexity.

05 — Multi-Site Operations

One partner. Multiple sites. One standard.

Consistency across multiple locations with centralised procurement, standardised SOPs and unified reporting. Ideal for organisations operating at scale across the city.

  • Centralised procurement
  • Standardised SOPs across sites
  • Unified reporting & dashboards
  • Single point of accountability
  • Scalable operations
The Benefit

Every site gets the same standard — no matter how many sites you add.

06 — Festive & Employee Experiences

Food as an employee experience.

Festive meals, themed menus, and engagement-led food experiences that bring teams together and celebrate cultural moments authentically.

  • Festival-specific menus (Durga Puja, Diwali, Eid, Christmas)
  • Themed food stations
  • Regional cuisine celebrations
  • Employee engagement tie-ins
  • Decoration & setup
The Benefit

Celebrations your employees remember. Operations your team doesn't have to manage.
