Our Story

We founded UR Hospitality in 2015 with a simple belief: corporate food should be as disciplined as the organisations it serves.

In our early years, we started like many others—catering for events and small offices. But we quickly realised that cooking good food was only half the battle. Delivering it consistently, on time, to hundreds of people simultaneously required a different kind of obsession.

We shifted our focus. We built a central kitchen, codified our recipes, and standardised our operating procedures. We stopped thinking of ourselves merely as cooks and started building the logistics required to manage dining at scale.

Today, we manage fully integrated corporate cafeteria operations for some of the world's most demanding companies—including Uber, Voith, INT, and Kolkata's leading corporates. Our evolution from a conventional caterer to an operational partner means that when our clients hire us, they aren't just buying lunch. They are buying the peace of mind that lunch will run perfectly, every single day.
