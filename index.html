11 — Our story

We started with food.
We became obsessed with getting the operation right.

Founded in 2015 in Kolkata, UR Hospitality began as a catering company with one clear belief: corporate food should be as disciplined as the organisations it serves.

Over ten years, we evolved — from catering to central kitchen operations, from one-off events to fully managed corporate cafeterias. Today, we serve organisations like Uber, Voith and INT with daily meal programs that run on structured SOPs, vetted supply chains and measured performance.

We're not the largest caterer in Kolkata. We're the most operationally reliable one. That's the reputation we've built, and the one we protect every day.
