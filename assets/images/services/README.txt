SERVICE BACKGROUND IMAGES
=========================

These images are used as very subtle (faded) backgrounds behind each service card in the "What We Offer" section.

Current images are from Unsplash (royalty-free).

Recommended: Replace these with your own high-quality photos of:
- Actual corporate cafeterias you manage
- Event setups
- Festive catering
- Pantry setups
- Large scale operations

Tips for replacement images:
- Use horizontal photos
- Keep them slightly desaturated if possible
- Maintain very low opacity (8-15%) so text remains readable
- Good resolution (minimum 800px width)

Current mapping:
1. Corporate Cafeteria Management → canteen-style photo
2. Corporate Events & Conferences → buffet / large spread
3. Wellness & Nutrition Programs → healthy Indian thali style
4. Themed & Festive Experiences → Indian festive food
5. Pantry & Micro-Kitchen Solutions → modern pantry / snacks
6. Multi-Location Enterprise Solutions → large scale dining

After replacing images, update the src paths in index.html under the Services section.
