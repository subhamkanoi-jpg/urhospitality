Guidelines for adding more photos:
- Use high-quality, well-lit photos
- Prefer horizontal (landscape) orientation
- Good examples: buffet spreads, live counters, clean canteen setups, plated meals, happy teams eating
- Avoid blurry or poorly lit photos

Recommended naming: gallery-06.jpeg, gallery-07.jpeg, etc.

After adding new images, update the Gallery section in index.html (search for "GALLERY SECTION").
